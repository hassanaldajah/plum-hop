Plum Hop

Roll, jump and survive across four hand-built worlds

How to play

  • The ball rolls where you steer it: ← → or A D, or the on-screen buttons. It keeps its momentum and rolls down slopes.
  • Press Space / ↑ / JUMP to jump. Hold it for a high jump, tap it for a small hop.
  • Collect all 30 gems, touch flags to save your spot, and reach the big flower. Progress is saved in this browser, so you can close the page and continue later.
  • Land on beetles, bees and birds from above to knock them out. Never touch a hedgehog: jump over it. Watch for icicles that drop when you pass under them. P or Esc pauses.
  • Later worlds add crumbling logs, slippery ice, dark crystal caves, wind gusts and updrafts that lift you.
Plum Hop is an original game, made as a loving tribute to the bouncing-ball platformers of the classic keypad-phone era.
