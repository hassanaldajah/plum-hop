Plum Hop

A bouncy little plum on a big adventure across four worlds

How to play

  • Plum never stops bouncing. Steer with ← → or A D, or the on-screen buttons.
  • Press Space / ↑ / JUMP just before landing for a big bounce. Hold it to keep bouncing high.
  • Collect all 30 gems, touch flags to save your spot, and reach the big flower. Progress is saved in this browser, so you can close the page and continue later.
  • Avoid thorns and pits. Hop on beetles from above. P or Esc pauses.
  • Later worlds add crumbling logs, slippery ice, dark crystal caves, wind gusts and updrafts that lift you.
Plum Hop is an original game, made as a loving tribute to the bouncing-ball platformers of the classic keypad-phone era.
